# HEXORA AI — Pilot Validation and Go-to-Market

## Positioning
"Mas mabilis gumawa ng maayos at consistent na social media content ang online sellers."

Avoid claims that the software guarantees sales, viral reach, or a specific return on investment.

## First customer segment
Start with Philippine online sellers who already post on Facebook/Instagram but struggle to prepare content consistently. Keep the first pilot narrow; do not target every industry at once.

## Discovery questions
1. How many posts do you prepare each week?
2. Which part takes the most time: ideas, captions, design, scheduling, or reporting?
3. What tools do you currently use?
4. What would make a content planner worth paying for monthly?
5. Would you test a simple workflow and provide feedback?

## Pilot offer
Offer a clearly scoped 7-day assisted pilot. Deliver a small content calendar and editable caption drafts. Obtain permission before using customer business names, logos, or content in examples.

## Metrics
- Interviews completed
- Pilot activation rate
- Time to first useful content plan
- Weekly active users
- Content drafts created/exported
- Repeat use after 7 days
- Willingness to pay
- Support minutes per customer
- AI cost per active customer
- Gross margin after payment and hosting costs

## First 30-day sequence
- Week 1: interview 5 sellers; record repeated pain points
- Week 2: build only the most requested content-planning workflow
- Week 3: run 3 assisted pilots; observe failures and confusion
- Week 4: ask for paid-pilot commitments; revise scope and pricing from evidence

## Customer acquisition channels
- Direct conversations with local online sellers
- MSD Digital Finds Facebook page and existing audience
- Helpful educational posts and short demonstrations
- Referrals from pilot customers

Do not send unsolicited bulk messages or scrape personal contact details.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Product works end-to-end in a test environment
- [ ] Privacy notice and terms are published
- [ ] Support contact and response expectations are clear
- [ ] Pricing and plan limits are accurate
- [ ] Payment and cancellation flows are tested
- [ ] Backup and incident-response procedures exist
- [ ] No unresolved critical/high security findings
- [ ] Customer onboarding and deletion flows are tested

## Revenue disclaimer
Revenue depends on customer demand, retention, pricing, costs, and execution. No income is guaranteed. Treat the proposed prices as experiments until customers validate them.
